The actual cost of a specific activity proposed in an application may vary depending on
factors such as travel distances, labour costs in different countries, involvement of
participants with fewer opportunities, choosing more environmentally sustainable
services and products, etc. This is why the applicants are requested to provide a
breakdown and explanation of their expected costs in the application form. The experts
should use the information provided by the applicant when assessing if higher than
average costs are justified, or if lower than average costs are realistic for a certain
activity.

In case of strong doubts about the value for money of an activity or the entire project,
where the professional judgment of the expert alone is not sufficient, or in case of
serious disagreement between two experts, a comparison with similar activities between
projects and with previous projects funded under the action Strategic Partnerships in
the Erasmus + programme 2014-2020 can be made as part of a consolidation exercise.
Using this reference shall only be used as a methodological support, using the necessary
degree of flexibility and proportionality to make the assessment fair and accurate.
