PARTNERSHIPS FOR EXCELLENCE

WHAT ARE PARTNERSHIPS FOR EXCELLENCE?

Partnerships for Excellence support projects with a long-term sustainable perspective. The following actions are
supported under this type of partnerships:

      •   Centres of Vocational Excellence (CoVEs)
      •   Erasmus+ Teacher Academies
      •   Erasmus Mundus Action:
              o Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters
              o Erasmus Mundus Design Measures.

These actions are managed by the European Education and Culture Executive Agency (EACEA).
