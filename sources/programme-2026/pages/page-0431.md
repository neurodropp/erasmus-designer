 Eligible costs - Specific conditions

 Eligible actual/real costs may be direct or indirect.

 Direct costs

 The eligible direct costs are specific costs directly linked to the performance of the action and which can therefore be
 booked to it directly. Please see Part B of this Guide for the budget categories reimbursed as actual costs.

 The beneficiary's internal accounting and auditing procedures must permit direct reconciliation of the costs and revenue
 declared in respect of the project with the corresponding accounting statements and supporting documents.
