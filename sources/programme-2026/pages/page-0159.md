AWARD CRITERIA
Submitted applications will be assessed by assigning points out of a total of 100, based on the below criteria and scores. To
be considered for award, applications must pass the following thresholds:

▪     At least 60 out of the total 100 points, and
▪     At least half of the maximum score points in each of the three award criteria below.

                                                   The extent to which:

                                                   •  the applicant’s profile, experience, activities and target population of
                                                      young people are relevant for the field of youth
                                                   • the project will address one or more of the priorities of the EU Youth
                                                      Dialogue or the Youth Goals,
                                                   • the project is suitable for contributing to the inclusion and diversity, green,
                                                      digital and participatory dimensions of the Programme;
                                                   • the project is relevant for the objectives of the Action
                                                   • the project is relevant for the respect and promotion of shared EU values,

                                                   • the project is likely to have an impact on participants and participating
