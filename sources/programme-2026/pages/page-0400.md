To be considered for funding, proposals must score at least 70 points. Furthermore, they must score at least 15 points in
each of the categories of the award criteria mentioned above.

Ex-aequo proposals will be prioritised according to the scores they have been awarded for the award criterion
‘Relevance’. When these scores are equal, priority will be based on their scores for the criterion ‘Quality of the project
design and implementation”. When these scores are equal, priority will be based on their scores for the criterion ‘Impact’.

If this does not allow to determine the priority, a further prioritisation can be done by considering the overall project
portfolio and the creation of positive synergies between projects, or other factors related to the objectives of the call.
These factors will be documented in the panel report.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Jean Monnet Learning EU Initiatives is expected to bring positive and long-lasting effects both to the schools and VET
providers and to the participants to their activities, in particular teachers and students.

The impact of Jean Monnet Learning EU Initiatives is measured through quantitative and qualitative indicators (see table
below), linked to the objectives of the Action which are described in the introductory section, “Objectives of the Action”.

Applicants are encouraged to use the following indicators in their applications, and to set their target values:
