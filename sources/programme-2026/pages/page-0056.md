 Duration of the   Staff mobility for teaching and training:
 mobility
 activity          The duration of mobility among EU Member States and countries associated to the Programme must
                   be from 2 to 60 days of physical mobility, excluding travel time. In the case of invited staff from
                   enterprises, the minimum duration is 1 day of physical mobility.

                   In case of mobility involving third countries not associated to the Programme, the duration must be
                   from 5 to 60 days, excluding travel time. The same applies for invited staff from enterprises.

                   In all cases, the minimum number of days must be consecutive.
