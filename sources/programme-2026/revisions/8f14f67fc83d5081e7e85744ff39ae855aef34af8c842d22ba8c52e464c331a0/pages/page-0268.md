EXPECTED IMPACT
The gradual establishment and development of European networks of Centres of Vocational Excellence is expected to
increase VET systems’ responsiveness to adapt skills provision to evolving economic and social needs, ensuring that VET
is at the forefront of providing solutions to the challenges posed by rapidly changing skills needs.

By forming an essential part of the “knowledge triangle” – projects should foster the collaboration between businesses,
education and research – and playing a fundamental role in providing skills to support innovation and smart
specialisation, the Centres of Vocational Excellence are expected to ensure high quality skills and competences that lead
to quality employment and career-long opportunities, which meet the needs of an innovative, inclusive and sustainable
economy.

development of a long-term action plan for the progressive roll out of project deliverables, taking national and regional
development and smart specialisation strategies into account, the projects are expected to engage relevant stakeholders
