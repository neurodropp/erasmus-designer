AWARD CRITERIA

Submitted applications will be assessed by assigning points out of a total of 100, based on the below criteria and scores. To
be considered for award, applications must pass the following thresholds:

▪      At least 60 out of the total 100 points, and
▪      At least half of the maximum score points in each of the three award criteria below:

                                                   The extent to which:
                                                   •    the applicant’s profile, experience and activities are relevant to the youth
                                                        work field
                                                   •    the project will address one or more of the priorities of the EU Youth
                                                        Dialogue or the Youth Goals;
                                                   •    the project is suitable for contributing to the inclusion and diversity, green,
                                                        digital and participatory dimensions of the Programme;
                                                   •    the project proposal is relevant for the objectives of the Action;
                                                   •    the project is relevant for the respect and promotion of shared EU values,
                                                        such as respect for human dignity, freedom, democracy, equality, the rule
                                                        of law and respect for human rights, as well as fighting any sort of
                                                        discrimination.
    Relevance, rationale and impact                •    the project and the proposed activities address the needs of development
    (maximum score 30 points)                           of the participating youth workers and youth work organisations;
                                                   •    the project is suitable for:
                                                             o reinforcing or transforming the participating organisations’
                                                                  practice, in relation to quality, innovation and recognition of
                                                                  youth work, as well as their scope, from local to global as
                                                                  appropriate.
                                                             o producing high-quality learning outcomes for participating youth
                                                                  workers;
                                                             o involving participants active in youth work in the participating
                                                                  organisations as well as organisations who undertake concrete
                                                                  youth work and regular work with young people on local level.
                                                             o producing impact on participating youth workers and
                                                                  participating organisations during and after the project lifetime.
                                                             o Producing impact outside the organisations and on individuals
                                                                  not directly participating in the project, at local, regional, national
                                                                  and/or European or global level.
