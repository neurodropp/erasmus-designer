Eligibility Criteria

                           A participating organisation can be:

                               •    A public or private organisation active in the sport and physical activity field
                                    and organising sport and physical activities at grassroots level (e.g. non-profit
                                    organisation, local public authority, sport club)
 Who can apply?
                               •    A non-grassroot sport organisation if its staff participation benefits
                                    grassroot sports (e.g., international organisation, national sport federation).

                           Applicant organisations must be established in an EU Member State or in a third
                           country associated to the Programme.

                           Applications are submitted to the National Agency in the country where the
 Where to apply?
                           applicant organisation is established.

                           Applicants must submit their grant applications by the following date:

                           12 February at 12:00:00 (midday Brussels time)

                           Possible additional round:
 When to apply?            National Agencies may decide to open an additional round. National Agencies will
                           inform the applicants about the opening of the additional round through their
                           website.

                           If an additional round is organised, applicants must submit their applications by 1
                           October at 12:00:00 (midday Brussels time).

                           Projects will start between 1 June and 31 December of the same year.
 Project start dates
                           If an optional round is opened, projects will start between 1 January and 31 May of
                           the following year.

 How to apply?             Please see part C of this Guide for details on how to apply.

 Duration of the project   From 3 to 18 months.

                           An organisation may submit only one application per selection round.
 Number of applications    Organisations that receive a grant for a project under the first round of applications
                           may not apply for the second round of the same call for proposals.
