WHAT ARE THE FUNDING RULES?

This action follows a lump sum funding model. This funding scheme would allow putting focus on the outputs rather than
the inputs, thereby placing emphasis on the quality and level of achievement of measurable objectives.


                                     The maximum EU grant per project is EUR 35 000.



The lump sum contributions will cover staff costs, travel and subsistence costs, equipment costs and subcontracting as
well as other costs (dissemination of information, publishing, translation).
