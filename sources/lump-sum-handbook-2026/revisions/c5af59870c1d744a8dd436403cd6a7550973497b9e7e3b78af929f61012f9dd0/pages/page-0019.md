    4.3. Quality assessment of the final report

The National Agency will perform a quality assessment of the implemented activities, and
the achieved results based on the final report submitted by the beneficiary.

Based on the final report, the National Agency attributes an overall score to the project,
calculated as the sum of the individual scores attributed to each of the criteria: relevance,
quality of the partnership, quality of the project implementation, and impact.
