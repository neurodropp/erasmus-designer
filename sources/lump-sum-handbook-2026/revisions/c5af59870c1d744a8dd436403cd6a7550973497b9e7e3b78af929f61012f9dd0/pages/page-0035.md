Annex 2: How to build your indicators
In addition to providing a framework for defining objectives, the European Commission
Better Regulation toolbox also provides a helpful tool for defining indicators:
https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/9c8d2189-8abd-4f29-84e9-
abc843cc68e0_en?filename=BR%20toolbox%20-%20Jul%202023%20-%20FINAL.pdf.

According to this framework the indictors should be R.A.C.E.R – as explained in the table
below.
