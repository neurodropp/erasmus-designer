                            ANNEX II – MULTIPLE
                              PARTICIPATIONS
 The table below provides an overview of the possible combinations of activities available for participants. Please
 refer to column 1 (in grey) as first activity carried out by a participant and the other columns as subsequent
 activities. The eligibility rules for each activity type are described in the relevant section of this guide.
 The total amount of time spent by a young person in European Solidarity Corps volunteering activities must not
 exceed 12 months and the activities carried out must not overlap. Activities carried out under Erasmus+
 volunteering/European Voluntary Service will count towards the total of 12 months allowed. Solidarity Projects
 are not included in the 12-month time limit.
