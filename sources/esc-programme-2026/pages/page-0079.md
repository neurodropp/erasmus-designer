Part C – Quality Label for Humanitarian Aid Volunteering




            MONITORING, REPORTING AND QUALITY ASSURANCE MEASURES

 CHANGING/UPDATING THE QUALITY LABEL
     The organisations holding a Quality Label for Humanitarian Aid Volunteering must notify the awarding body
     of any changes in their organisation that should be reflected in their Quality Label.

 MONITORING AND CHECKS
     The Executive Agency may carry out regular or ad hoc monitoring visits to verify that the organisations holding
     a Quality Label still meet the quality standards.

 TERMINATION
     In cases of serious underperformance, failure to comply with Executive Agency’s instructions and deadlines
     or repeated violations of the Programme rules, the Quality Label for Humanitarian Aid Volunteering can be
     withdrawn. The Executive Agency will give due consideration to each case of non-compliance and will prompt
     the organisation well in advance to allow enough time to take corrective measures.
     In addition, the Executive Agency may terminate a Quality Label if the organisation ceases to exist.
