AWARD CRITERIA
To be considered for funding, proposals must score at least 60 points. Furthermore, they must score at least half
of the maximum points in each of the categories of award criteria mentioned below.
In ex aequo cases, priority will be given to highest scores for "Relevance, rationale and impact" and then “Quality
of project management” and then “Quality of project design”.
