i       According to Article 33.3 of the Council Decision 2013/755/EU on the Association of the OCTs with the European Union, the Union shall ensure that
        individuals and organisations from or to Overseas Countries and Territories (OCT) shall be eligible to participate in the European Solidarity Corps on an “EU
        Member States or third countries associated to the Programme status”, subject to the rules of the Programme and the arrangements applicable to the
        Member State with which these OCTs are connected. The list of OCTs can be found at: https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/countries/overseas-
        countries-and-territories_en.
ii      Subject to the signature of the Association Agreements between the European Union and those countries.
iii     This designation is without prejudice to positions on status and is in line with UNSCR 1244 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo declaration of independence.
iv      In line with the Council Conclusions of 12 October 2020 and in light of Belarus’s involvement in the Russian military aggression against Ukraine, recognised
        in the European Council Conclusions of February 2022, the EU has stopped engaging with representatives of Belarus public bodies and state-owned
        enterprises. Should there be a change of the context this may be reconsidered. In the meantime, the EU continues to engage with and, where possible, has
        stepped up support to non-state, local and regional actors, including within the framework of this programme as appropriate. For security reasons, while
        mobilities of young people from Belarus can be allowed, no mobilities to Belarus are allowed.
v       In line with the Council Conclusions of 19 December 2024 and in response to political developments in Georgia, the EU has reconsidered financial
        assistance directly benefitting the Georgian authorities. Taking into account Article 14(2) of the Regulation (EU) 2021/888 of the European Parliament and
        of the Council of 20 May 2021 establishing the European Solidarity Corps Programme, direct financial assistance from the Programme to Georgian
        authorities - which should be understood as central government, local authorities, agencies and state-owned enterprises - is not considered in the Union’s
        interest. Georgian authorities are thus not eligible under any of the actions under this guide.
vi      The eligibility criteria formulated in commission notice Nr.2013/C-205/05 (OJEU C-205 of 19/07/2013, pp. 9-11) shall apply for all actions implemented
        through this Programme Guide, including with respect to third parties receiving financial support in the cases where the respective action involves
        financial support to third parties by grant beneficiaries in accordance with Article 207 of the EU's Financial Regulation.
vii     This designation shall not be construed as recognition of a State of Palestine and is without prejudice to the individual positions of the Member States on
        this issue.
