# Part C - Information for applicants

All potential applicants who intend to submit a project proposal in order to receive financial support from the EU under the Erasmus+ Programme are invited to carefully read this section which is drafted in accordance with the "EU Financial Regulation"
  1

.

All the contractual and financial provisions that apply to awarded grants are presented in the grant agreements, which are made available for projects implemented by the Executive Agency (EACEA) in the EU Funding & Tenders Portal and for projects implemented by the National Agencies on the website of the relevant Erasmus+ National Agency. In case of discrepancies with the information presented in this Guide, provisions of the grant agreements prevail over those of Part C.
