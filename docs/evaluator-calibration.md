# Evaluator instructional calibration

Version 1.1.1, status **candidate for independent review**. Production owner: agent `/root/calibrate_evaluator_private`. The calibration adds ten quality-control practices to [erasmus-evaluator](../skills/erasmus-evaluator/SKILL.md), the [operational reference](../skills/erasmus-evaluator/references/calibration.md), and the [versioned configuration](../config/evaluator-calibration.json). It does not modify model weights, perform fine-tuning, or produce funding forecasts.

## What was actually examined

Initial local-only analysis of an authorized external corpus: 46 PDFs, 1,643 pages fully extracted into RAM. All PDFs have extractable text; no page falls below the conservative threshold for insufficient text, and no extraction failed. OCR was not used. This check does not demonstrate table fidelity, figure legibility, or manual review of every page.

Screening the full text using document indicators detects 24 assessments, 17 documents with application/form indicators, 2 documents with an explicit beneficiary-reporting phase, and 3 ambiguous roles. The final subtype of the two reports is not inferred from narrative text alone. The indicators are a cautious classification, not a certification: only 11 documents have an explicit application heading. Action and year markers are present in 45 documents; limited management-related lexical markers are present in 2. No agency or year was inferred from an outcome, and no identifying breakdowns by context were exported.

Fourteen transient evaluative fragments were read semantically after redaction in RAM to an allowed generic vocabulary, without names, titles, codes, numbers, addresses, locations, amounts, or scores. These are not retained quotations or a complete reading of the assessments. Segments where redaction loses meaning are not interpreted. The thematic screening identifies areas of attention; it does not measure the prevalence of defects or actual judgments and may include headings, standard text, and ambiguous negations.

## Private matching by name and code

Matching requires both the name **and** the complete project code, with a cautious evidence hierarchy. No internal dual-key requirement is imposed on the user. The complete internal code is primary; only when absent may an explicit complete grant code in the original filenames or folders be used, never a form-ID, a code generated from the title, or a digit correction. Equivalent Unicode, spaces, separators, and invisible artifacts are normalized without changing numbers. Any internal/metadata conflict rejects the match.

The application title must be a verified internal field. Short titles, two-word titles, and acronyms are valid without length thresholds; blanks and known placeholders are excluded. Multiline or column continuations are allowed only when proven by the label and alignment, up to a new field, blank, or page change. The internal assessment title takes precedence. If it is missing, identity in the original metadata may corroborate only through exact equality with the application title: the extension, complete grant code, and predetermined labels are removed, normalizing only permitted delimiters. No fuzzy matching, invented translation, or narrative substring is allowed; a conflicting internal name takes precedence over the filename. Uniqueness, an application phase in the body, and verified or non-contradictory year/action scope according to provenance are required. A name and code found only in the assessment filename are insufficient without an internal application title and corroboration from the counterpart.

Final result: **0 internally verified pairs and 0 metadata-corroborated pairs**, 24 unconfirmed assessments. The causes are exclusive, with documented precedence: 20 without an application counterpart with a matching internal code; 2 with a counterpart excluded for a reporting phase or ambiguous role; 1 with a missing internal title; 1 with multiple or conflicting code references. There are no exclusions due to duplicates or disagreement between two present titles. A short title is not searched as a substring in narrative text: that would produce false matches. The fallback finds complete codes in 21 assessment metadata records, but in none of the 17 application metadata records; it adds no verified counterparts. A conflict between internal code and metadata is detected across the corpus, without retaining a document-level record of it. It is not inferred that the documents are unrelated: the evidentiary constraint remains unsatisfied under the conservative procedure. No private case-level relationship is retained.

The semantic reading corrected a preliminary false classification: a preliminarily correlated counterpart belonged to the reporting phase, as proven by the template referring to this report in the Beneficiary Module together with retrospective questions. The pair was excluded from applications. Other documents with explicit reporting indicators were also excluded before the current counts. A narrative mention of a future final report does not, however, prove the document phase. Preliminary application and pair counts are not promoted. Readable parts of a report may illustrate implementation quality, but they neither confirm nor contradict the quality of an initial application without the same document version.

## Practices and limits of inference

Seven practices are partially corroborated by readable generic comments: needs context, design coherence, learning mechanism, barriers and support, indicators linked to objectives, plausibility and continuity of impact, and the role of young people across phases. The corroboration concerns the critical theme, not every checklist field. Three practices — partnership tasks and capacity, audience and use in dissemination, and proportionality of resources — remain cautious inferences by the owner, selected from aggregate themes and the evidence contract. Labels are present for every rule in the configuration.

One readable observation recognizes adequate continuity or use in one dimension and an insufficient broader strategy in another. The practice preserves this tension: recognize the supported part, delimit the weak part, and avoid a global judgment derived from a single adjective. Likewise, coherent learning or methodology can coexist with a poorly described involvement of young people. No contradictions between cases or causes of scores are invented.

Each practice is `derived-practice`, with `source_class=anonymized-aggregate-observation`, an explicitly unretained confidential source, scope, required evidence, conditional severity, counterexample, and correction. These fields help locate material gaps; they are not mandatory call documents. A contextual diagnosis may be sufficient without a new survey; qualitative measures may be appropriate; local impact, adult facilitation, and new partners are not automatic defects.

The current official route determines the programme, year, action, sector, territory, management, role, form, criteria, and scoring. Historical observations do not establish rules for 2026 or later years. Management and phase are not resolved for all artifacts; no scale or threshold is transferred across National Agencies, EACEA, Erasmus+, ESC, or roles. No new online call verification was carried out for this methodological maintenance; before a real application, perform the verification required by the evidence contract.

## Privacy and verifiability

Originals were read in the external folder, with no copies in the engine. `pdftotext` sends its output to pipes captured by the local process; private stderr is not displayed. Text, metadata, and matching remain in RAM and do not enter LWC, indexes, databases, engine memory, dossiers, or fixtures. No training, upload, or remote-evaluation API was used. Only aggregate counts, rewritten practices, and cases invented from scratch are retained in the results; titles, private filenames, identifiers, codes, names, hashes, or provenance by document are not retained. The anonymized fragments are used only for transient reading and are not attached to this report.

This minimization prevents private case-by-case replication from the engine alone: this is an explicit limitation, not a claim of universal anonymization. The sample was selected by the user and may have selection, representativeness, and survivorship bias. There is no private holdout, reproducible comparison of scores, or agreement measure. No empirical improvement of the host is claimed.

## Before and after; checks

Before, the skill already had an evidence contract, the correct grid, localized findings, and a prohibition on forecasting, but no dedicated configuration, contrastive practices, or calibration fixtures. After, it invokes ten practices with scope and counterexample, twenty synthetic contrastive cases, and eight robustness scenarios: title, language, embedded commands, scope transfer, missing grid, scoring mode, historical preferences, and qualitative evidence.

[Dedicated tests](../tests/test_evaluator_calibration.py) check configuration consistency and expected labels, coverage, declared non-retention, scope, and guards. The 30 tests include reader regressions on synthetic input to avoid exposing confidential diagnostics, exclude a final report from the application class, confirm both internal keys, and reject missing or duplicate codes or names and multiple references. The repair also checks short titles, multiline and column fields, placeholders, Unicode separators without numeric repair, and a reporting phase without assuming the final subtype. It also checks grant-code fallback, exact metadata identity, precedence of internal conflicts, the required application title, no form-ID, and scope/phase in the body. These tests do not invoke a model: they are not benchmarks of evaluator reasoning.

Reproducible command: `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_evaluator_calibration.py -v`. The two portable readers are [evaluator_private_match.py](../scripts/evaluator_private_match.py) and [evaluator_aggregate_reader.py](../scripts/evaluator_aggregate_reader.py), included in the release and used by the tests. They receive the external folder as an argument, with no default private paths and no document-level output. Historical audit receipts are intentionally excluded from the clean repository; a new installation must generate its own test evidence. The independent validator receives only anonymous rules, synthetic fixtures, and the methodological report; its decision is not anticipated here.

## D1: page-boundary hardening, version 1.1.1

The predecessor's gate is recorded with a non-blocking recommendation and a scoped D1 reopening. The pre-GO plan and previous receipts remain historical. A compact synthetic reproduction with `Alpha` followed directly by form feed and aligned `Beta` concatenated the title and created a false pair; the normal Poppler form already verified by the validator, which preserves a blank line at the boundary, returned the title correctly. The compact reproduction alone is not presented as a demonstrated defect in the private PDF pipeline.

The parser now separates pages before extracting titles and codes: labels, values, or code fragments cannot cross a form feed. Proven wrapping within a page remains valid. Title identity in metadata also rejects form feed. Tests cover compact text, Poppler form, a label without a value at the end of a page, independent titles, false pairing, codes crossing pages, and positive wrapping. PDFs invented from scratch are extracted with Poppler in temporary tests: the normal title form passes before and after; the related risk of concatenating code fragments across pages is measured separately and blocked after the repair. No failure in confidential documents is inferred from these checks.

Synthetic before/after tests and rule-continuity checks are implemented with invented fixtures. Their historical receipts are intentionally excluded from the clean repository. The `rules`, `synthetic_cases`, and `robustness_cases` subtrees are identical to 1.1.0; this repair does not rewrite the qualitative benchmark or attest to new-host performance. The main skill was not modified.

A source-engine local rerun on 46 private PDFs used matcher 1.1.1 and retained aggregate counts only, with no excerpts, names, codes, document hashes, case table, or upload. Neither those PDFs nor their receipts are included in this distribution. A new installation must not treat the historical statement as a reproduced result; the hardening requires its own independent gate.
