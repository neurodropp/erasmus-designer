## Destination

A local assistant that designs, writes, and evaluates Erasmus+ applications using a wiki organized by action and sector, the two supplied 2026 PDFs, EU sources, and Italian provisions, with verifiable evidence.

## Notes

Local Markdown tracker; tickets are the files `docs/decisions/*.md`, with parent, assignee, status, and blocking information in the frontmatter. In this assignment, the user asks to create the system: the map explicitly includes implementation. A separate planning-only session is not required. Wayfinder is used as a decision map, adapting unavailable tools to the local tracker. No external issues are published. The identically named research skill is unavailable: use official web research and local documents. Preserve conclusions and evidence, not internal reasoning.

## Decisions so far

- [Application template](decisions/application-template.md): complete question map, per-instance limits, tables/examples, and conflicts in the 2025 template.
- [Annexes](decisions/annexes.md): 26 acquired profiles, 15 blueprints with a specific owner, and a common data model.
- [ESC](decisions/esc-2026.md): verified guides, seven routes, and criteria and priorities kept distinct from Erasmus.
- [Integrated workflow](../wiki/topics/workflow-erasmus-esc.md): ten phases with critique, intermediate checks, and freezing of text/data/forms/annexes.

- [Architecture](decisions/architecture.md): canonical LWC, original evidence for each page in a rebuildable index, seven skills, and a Codex multi-agent cycle with bounded reviews.
- [Italian priorities](decisions/italian-priorities.md): provisions distinguished by agency, sector, action, and round; no automatic extension to Sport or centrally managed actions.
- [Good practices](decisions/good-practices.md): requirements, criteria, methods, and recommendations kept distinct, with source conflicts preserved.

The system is built. This closure concerns infrastructure and research, not the validation of a real application. Any excluded private audit evidence is not shipped with this map.

## Not yet specified

The focus of the first applications and an organization's real data will be specified through use; they do not prevent building the complete 2026 corpus.

## Out of scope

Submitting applications, contacting partners, and publishing materials. No credentials or paid service are needed for local research.
