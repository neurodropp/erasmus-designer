## Question

Which official 2026 documents establish the priorities and provisions of INDIRE, INAPP, and AIG? How can we avoid applying a priority to the wrong sector or action?

## Resolution

Resolved on 12 September 2026 through research based exclusively on official sources. Maintain separate priority records by year, sector, action, and receiving authority; distinguish explicit scopes from interpretive links, and tie-break rules from thematic priorities.

The research is documented in [Italian Erasmus+ priorities for 2026 by sector and action](../../wiki/research/priorita-italiane-2026.md), including sources, PDF page references, limitations, and additional URLs to retrieve.

INDIRE SCH/ADU: KA210/KA220, 2026 PDF, p. 8. INDIRE HE: KA220-HED, version 2 dated May, p. 9; the tie-break rule is separate. INAPP: national VET list dated 24 February, pp. 1–2; the link to partnerships is interpretive and based on the 2026 FAQ. AIG: two national priorities for KA220-YOU under the 5 March deadline, in the 26 February news item. Sport: AIG receives KA182 applications; Sport partnerships are handled by EACEA.

Do not apply the two AIG priorities to the entire programme; do not invent numerical bonuses or transfer ranking criteria from one agency or sector to another.
