## Question

Which operational practices are supported by the 2026 guides, official manuals, and Erasmus+ quality resources? Distinguish requirements, interpretations, and editorial recommendations.

## Resolution

Adopt a set of practices explicitly classified as requirements, quality criteria, recommended methods, or derived recommendations; retain their sources, programme year, action, and limitations. The research covers cross-cutting principles and provides detail for youth KA1, KA210, and KA220 actions managed by National Agencies, without generalizing financial rules or scoring grids to other actions.

- [Documented good practices](../../wiki/research/buone-pratiche-application.md): needs analysis, European added value, intervention logic, indicators, partnership, priorities, youth learning, budget, safeguarding, impact, and AI integrity.
- [Reasoned review template](../../wiki/templates/revisione-application.md): eligibility kept separate from quality, mapping to the actual criteria, and missing evidence.

Key decisions: use Programme Guide p.243 for the KA220 management cost ceiling of 20% of the total; preserve the inconsistency in Expert Guide p.70. For KA210, 20% is a flexible reference, not an automatic limit. The 2026 Handbook was verified on the Slovak National Agency's domain. Distinguish the historical UK Impact+ Exercise, which was not retrieved, from the separate project output “Impact Plus Guide” hosted by SALTO. Funded examples are sources of inspiration subject to survivor bias, not evaluation grids.

No new blocking decision arose from this research. Updating sources and applying them to the specific call remain necessary checks for every project.
