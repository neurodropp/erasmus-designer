## Question

Which sources, actions, roles, physical PDF page ranges, evaluation grids, and Italian provisions for ESC 2026 must be indexed separately from Erasmus+?

## Resolution

Resolved on 12 September 2026 by reading the attached PDFs and checking official sources. The two copies are identical by SHA-256 to the Commission's current PDFs. Keep `program=ESC` in every record and retrieve only sources relevant to the programme, year, action, and role.

The map covers seven routes: ESC50 host/support, ESC50 lead, ESC51, ESC30, high-priority groups, the humanitarian aid label, and humanitarian aid volunteering. The grids distinguish pass/fail, additional lead scoring, ESC51 allocation, and centrally managed calls; the attached expert guide covers ESC50/ESC30 only. Erasmus priorities are not transferred to ESC.

The evidence, page ranges, and limitations are in [European Solidarity Corps 2026 - actions, sources, and evaluation](../../wiki/research/esc-2026-routing.md). Integrable assets: [actions](../../config/esc-actions.json), [sources](../../config/esc-sources.json). The catalogue retains seven acquired sources, confirmed Italian deadlines, and the distinction between the humanitarian aid label submitted in 2026 and the 2027 grant.

The ticket resolves the research and classification model. The main phase integrates the catalogue and rebuilds the index; checking centrally managed topics and the live application form remains necessary for each specific application.
