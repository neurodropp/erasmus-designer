## Humanitarian Quality Label: support

Profile `esc-humaid-ql-support-2026`. Selector: `{"role": "support"}`; no default at the first variant.

Method: qualitative. Meet the published quality criteria for the support role. No numerical maximum, 60/100 threshold or subscore observed in the Local Guide.

Sources: esc-programme-2026 PDF 71–75; esc-programme-2026 PDF 76–78.

Contesto necessario: role.

### Relevance

Sources: esc-programme-2026 PDF 76.

#### esc-humaid-ql-support.relevance.motivation

- [ ] Why does the organization want to play this humanitarian role?
