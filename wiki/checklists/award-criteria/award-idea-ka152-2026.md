# Award criteria — idea checklist: KA152

Corpus2026; ERASMUS, YOU sectors, AIG agency.

Route-specific methodological checklist. At the idea phase evaluate relevance/plausibility, available evidence and gap plan; not awarding official grades or declaring all final criteria met. Before applying, check the call/corrigenda, national provisions and actual forms. Internal check IDs, physical PDF pages1-based.

Outcomes for each check: evidence-supported, plausible, research-needed, redesign-needed, not-applicable motivated; keep proof, reason, plan and owner. Unresolved selector or incompatible sector/role prevents the gate on the chosen rubric; non-selective details can remain research with credible plan.

### Relevance, rationale and impact — maximum 30

Sources: program-2026 PDF 159; expert-2026 PDF 21–25.

#### na-ka152-relevance-profile

- [ ] What ordinary work with young people or youth workers makes the applicant relevant to the chosen route?

Rewritten official focus: Relevance of youth camp applicant's profile, experience, activities and youth target population/groups.
Scope: All routes and profile variations.
Try the idea phase: IDEA note with the applicant's activities, experience and real recipients; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document the applicant's activities, experience and real recipients, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
