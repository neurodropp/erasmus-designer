# Award criteria — idea checklist: KA171

Corpus2026; ERASMUS, HED sectors, INDIRE agency.

Route-specific methodological checklist. At the idea phase evaluate relevance/plausibility, available evidence and gap plan; not awarding official grades or declaring all final criteria met. Before applying, check the call/corrigenda, national provisions and actual forms. Internal check IDs, physical PDF pages1-based.

Outcomes for each check: evidence-supported, plausible, research-needed, redesign-needed, not-applicable motivated; keep proof, reason, plan and owner. Unresolved selector or incompatible sector/role prevents the gate on the chosen rubric; non-selective details can remain research with credible plan.

## HED mobility with external policy funds: project control and for regional partnership

Profile `na-ka171`. Selector: `{}`; no default at the first variant.

Mode: points. 40/40/20 grid: quality of design is project-specific, strategy and impact are assessed for each regional partnership; each region must achieve 60/100 and half of the maximum for each criterion. No IDEA scores. Do not use the HED Consortium Accreditation Grid (pp.44–46) as the KA171 Universal Grid.

### Quality of the project design and cooperation arrangements — maximum 40

Sources: program-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 16–17.

#### na-ka171-design-roles

- [ ] Who selects, prepares, hosts, finances and recognizes mobilities?

Rewritten official focus: Clarity of responsibilities, roles and tasks between partners.
Scope: Entire project: response and control only once, even for consortia.
Try the idea phase: IDEA note with an initial matrix of applicant and partner tasks and resources; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document an initial matrix of the tasks and resources of the applicant and partner, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Consequence on design: Distribute tasks and communication channels before estimating flows.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 16–17.

#### na-ka171-design-values

- [ ] Which selection and cooperation choices protect dignity, freedom, democracy, equality, the rule of law and human rights?

Rewritten official focus: Relevance to the respect and promotion of EU values ​​and the fight against any discrimination.
Scope: Entire project, including partner institutions in non-associated countries.
Test at the idea phase: IDEA note with proposed choices for fair access and protection of EU values; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document proposed choices for fair access and protection of EU values, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design consequence: Integrating EU values ​​into mobility processes.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 16–17.

#### na-ka171-design-selection-support-recognition

- [ ] How will students and staff be selected and supported and achievements recognized in both institutions?

Rewritten official focus: Completeness and quality of selection, support and recognition of the mobility period, especially in the non-associated country.
Scope: Entire project; specify the mechanisms for each type of mobility and country.
Try the idea phase: IDEA note with an inclusive selection draft, logistical and linguistic support and recognition of learning; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document a draft of inclusive selection, logistical and linguistic support and recognition of learning, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Implication: Provide learning arrangements, supports and recognition before mobility.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 16–17.

### Relevance of the strategy — maximum 40

Sources: program-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.

#### na-ka171-strategy-internationalisation

- [ ] What internationalization objective does this regional partnership address for applicants and partners?

Rewritten official focus: Relevance of mobility to the internationalization strategy of the institutions involved.
Scope: Any regional partnership, including from the perspective of non-associated partners.
Test at the idea phase: IDEA note with objectives of the internationalization strategies of both parties and their connection to the flows; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document the objectives of the internationalization strategies of both parties and their connection to the flows, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Consequence on design: Define a mobility logic distinct by region.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.

#### na-ka171-strategy-student-staff

- [ ] Why do you need staff, students or both for this region, and in what volume?

Rewritten official focus: Reason for choosing staff and/or student mobility.
  Scope: Ogni partnership regionale.
Test at the idea phase: IDEA note with motivation of the profiles, proposed volumes and capacity of the institutions; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document the motivation of the profiles, the volumes proposed and the capacity of the institutions, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Impact: Adapt flows to real-world strategy and capabilities.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.

#### na-ka171-strategy-countries

- [ ] Why are these countries and institutions relevant to the proposed regional cooperation?

Rewritten official focus: Motivation for cooperation with institutions/organizations in specific countries in the region.
Scope: Any regional partnership; do not omit the specific choice of countries.
Proof at the idea stage: IDEA note with reasons for choosing countries and institutions, with previous experience if available; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document reasons for the choice of countries and institutions, with previous experience if available, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design consequence: Justifying geography with cooperative needs and goals.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.

### Impact and dissemination — maximum 20

Sources: program-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.

#### na-ka171-impact-impact

- [ ] What plausible changes will this regional partnership produce during and after the project?

Rewritten official focus: Potential impact on participants, applicants and partners at local, regional and national levels.
Scope: Any regional partnership, in all countries involved.
Test at the idea phase: IDEA note with expected changes and first monitoring proposal for people and institutions; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document expected changes and first monitoring proposal for people and institutions, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design consequence: Separating mobility products and effects in institutions.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.

#### na-ka171-impact-dissemination

- [ ] Who will use the results in the faculty, institution and beyond and through which channels?

Rewritten official focus: Quality of dissemination at faculty and institution level and beyond, where relevant, in all countries involved.
Scope: Any regional partnership; external staircase proportionate to the relevance.
Try the idea phase: IDEA note with audiences, shareable results and dissemination channels for applicants and partners; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document audiences, shareable results and dissemination channels of applicants and partners, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Impact: Assign recipients and reuse channels for each region.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 62; expert-2026 PDF 17.
