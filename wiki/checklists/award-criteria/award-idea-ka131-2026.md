### Prerequisite/allocation checks — not award criteria

#### na-ka131-idea-control-prior-quality

- [ ] Which valid ECHE or consortium accreditation supports the request for funds?

Official focus rewritten: IDEA control NON award: No quality assessment or new award grid: quality already assessed with ECHE or consortium accreditation.
Scope: All routes and profile variations.
Proof at idea stage: IDEA note with relevant quality title and validity to be verified; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document the relevant quality title and validity to be verified, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Implication: Check the title and use a mobility consistency check, without assigning new scores.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 61.

#### na-ka131-idea-control-requested-mobilities

- [ ] How many mobilities, for what profiles and with what management capacity are they proposed?

Rewritten official focus: IDEA NON award control: The maximum amount depends on the number of mobilities requested.
Scope: All routes and profile variations.
Test at the idea phase: IDEA note with preliminary volumes of mobility and capacity of the institutions; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document preliminary volumes of mobility and capacity of the institutions, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Implication: Estimate realistic flows consistent with titles and capacity.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 61.

#### na-ka131-idea-control-past-performance

- [ ] What previous data supports the ability to manage the proposed flows?

Official focus rewritten: IDEA NON award check: The amount takes into account mobility achieved, quality and previous financial management if the applicant has received similar funding.
Scope: Only if the applicant has received similar grants in previous years.
Test at the idea stage: Note IDEA with performance available on volumes, quality and financial management; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document available performance on volumes, quality and financial management, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Impact: Calibrate flows using real world performance if available.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 61.

#### na-ka131-idea-control-blended-intensive

- [ ] Which BIPs are proposed and how do they contribute to the strategy and capacity of the institutions?

Rewritten official focus: IDEA NON award control: The amount also depends on the number of blended intensive programs requested.
Scope: If BIPs are expected; no obligation to enter them.
Test at the idea phase: IDEA note with preliminary number and justification of the proposed BIPs; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document the preliminary number and justification of the proposed BIPs, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Implication: Motivate BIPs as a component of the mobility plan.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 61.

#### na-ka131-idea-control-national-budget

- [ ] Which 2026 national budget or rule should be verified to prudently estimate flows?

Rewritten official focus: IDEA NON award control: The amount depends on the total national budget allocated to mobility with internal funds.
Scope: All routes and profile variations.
Test at the idea phase: Note IDEA with candidate national source and prudent estimate of flows; distinguish available evidence, proposal and hypothesis, and indicate the subsequent collection needed.
Subsequent verification: In the design and in the form, verify and document the candidate national source and prudent estimate of the flows, updating the hypotheses with confirmed data and responsibilities.
Design Consequence: Check NA rules before setting expected funding.
Source: programme-2026 PDF 61.
