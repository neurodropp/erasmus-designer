### Prerequisite/allocation checks — not award criteria

#### esc51.allocation.label-plan

- [ ] Does the allocation idea fall within the label and the approved plan or does it require an update first?

Rewritten official focus: Simplified application based on activities already approved in the Quality Label lead; label valid upon expiry.
Scope: ESC51; allocation criteria/programming, not awards. AIG/Italy where indicated.
Try the idea phase: Known status of the label and initial correspondence of the activities, or path to verify them. Hypotheses stated with sources to be consulted, method, person in charge and proportionate verification times are permitted.
Subsequent verification: Check the validity of the lead label and activities approved at the time of application.
Design Consequence: Use the approved plan as a dependency, without rewriting it in the simplified application.
Source: esc-programme-2026 PDF 53; esc-aig-allocation-2026 PDF 3.

#### esc51.allocation.requested-activities

- [ ] What volume of activity is plausible and consistent with capacity and plan?

Rewritten official focus: Estimate of number, type and duration of activities and number of participants.
Scope: ESC51; allocation criteria/programming, not awards. AIG/Italy where indicated.
Test at the idea stage: Initial ranges of activities, duration, participants and capacity. Hypotheses stated with sources to be consulted, method, person in charge and proportionate verification times are permitted.
Subsequent verification: Verify demand estimates and operational capacity; activities must follow the rules of their type.
Design Impact: Scale the request to approved activities and capacity.
Source: esc-programme-2026 PDF 53–54; esc-aig-allocation-2026 PDF 3.

#### esc51.allocation.performance

- [ ] What past performance data are available, and what scale would avoid underuse?

#### esc51.allocation.non-associated-limit

- [ ] Does the hypothesized composition require quota control and exceptions?

Official focus rewritten: AIG recalls limit 25% of budget for participants from non-associated third countries, with exceptions published for Ukrainian nationality and projects up to 4 volunteers; criterion to be respected already in the application.
Scope: AIG/Italy, if participants interested in the quota are involved; verify the scope and relevance of the exceptions, without extending them to other routes.
Try the idea phase: Initial composition by origin and days/costs scenario. Hypotheses stated with sources to be consulted, method, person in charge and proportionate verification times are permitted.
Subsequent check: Check limit, exceptions, calculation required in the form and applicable version of the guide/call.
Consequence on the design: Size participants and days in compliance with the applicable constraints.
Source: esc-aig-allocation-2026 PDF 4.

#### esc51.allocation.in-country-limit

- [ ] Is the mix of in-country and transnational activities compatible with the constraint?

Rewritten official focus: In the AIG PDF the in-country volunteer fee cannot exceed 20% of the approved budget.
Scope: AIG/Italy and in-country activities; constraint control, not award criterion.
Test at the idea phase: Initial mix and share estimate of in-country activities. Hypotheses stated with sources to be consulted, method, person in charge and proportionate verification times are permitted.
Subsequent verification: Verify calculation and 20% constraint according to guidance, provisions and actual calls.
Design consequence: Sizing the mix of activities and resources to the applicable constraint.
Source: esc-aig-allocation-2026 PDF 4.
