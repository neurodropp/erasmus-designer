# Award criteria — idea checklist: CBVET

Corpus2026; ERASMUS, VET sectors, EACEA agency.

Route-specific methodological checklist. At the idea phase evaluate relevance/plausibility, available evidence and gap plan; not awarding official grades or declaring all final criteria met. Before applying, check the call/corrigenda, national provisions and actual forms. Internal check IDs, physical PDF pages1-based.

Outcomes for each check: evidence-supported, plausible, research-needed, redesign-needed, not-applicable motivated; keep proof, reason, plan and owner. Unresolved selector or incompatible sector/role prevents the gate on the chosen rubric; non-selective details can remain research with credible plan.

## Capacity building VET

Profile `central-cbvet-2026`. Selector: `{}`; no default at the first variant.

Mode: points. Application thresholds: Relevance 15/30; Design 15/30; Partnerships 10/20; Impact 10/20. No IDEA scoring or subpoint weighting; IDs are internal, not official. Ex aequo: Relevance, then Quality of the project design and implementation, then Impact.

Final Application: maximum 100; total threshold 60. The thresholds per criterion and the ex aequo remain those specified above; no votes at the idea stage.

Context to check: region. At the idea stage the common rubric can be selected with this data still open; fill out methodological checks with owner, reason and credible plan. These are not new award criteria.
