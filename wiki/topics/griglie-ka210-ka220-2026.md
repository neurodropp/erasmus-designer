# KA210 and KA220 scoring grids — 2026

This summary uses the 2026 v1 program guide. Before applying, check the version and actual call. The limits are per criterion; the subpoints of the expert guide guide the judgment and do not receive separate official scores.

| Criterion | KA210 maximum / minimum | KA220 maximum / minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | 30 / 15 | 25 / 13 |
| Quality of design and implementation | 30 / 15 | 30 / 15 |
| Quality of partnership and cooperation | 20 / 10 | 20 / 10 |
| Impact | 20 / 10 | 25 / 13 |
| Minimum total | 60 / 100 | 70 / 100 |

Sources: [KA210, PDF program guide pp.248–249](../../raw/pdf/programme-guide-2026-en.pdf) — evidence `programme-2026:p0248`, `programme-2026:p0249`; [KA220, PDF pp.240–242](../../raw/pdf/programme-guide-2026-en.pdf) — `programme-2026:p0240`, `programme-2026:p0241`, `programme-2026:p0242`. For criteria worth 25 points, the minimum is 13: half is rounded up to the next whole point. Do not automatically use grids from previous years.

## Evaluation simulation

The [expert guide PDF pp.9–11](../../raw/pdf/expert-guide-2026-v1.pdf) requires judgment on the information present, proportionality and integer votes. For calls handled by national agencies, also use pp.45–57 (KA220) or pp.58–64 (KA210). For Sport EACEA read specific call/form: the attached national expert guide does not have that scope.

A simulated score can indicate weaknesses against a criterion, supported by textual evidence and accompanied by uncertainty. Exceeding the thresholds and minimum scores makes a proposal eligible for consideration for funding; it does not guarantee selection, available resources or ranking. Merely mentioning a priority does not automatically earn a numerical bonus.
