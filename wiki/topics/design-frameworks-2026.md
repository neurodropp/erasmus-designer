# Framework di design, logica di intervento e award criteria

Internal procedure authorized by the user on 14 September 2026. Portable Executive Contract: `docs/design-methodology.md` in engine root. Policy and eight `method-*` controls are from the engine, without introducing new tender requirements. Textual paths `docs/` and `config/` resolve from the common root; plans `design/` and `application/` from the assigned file.

Participatory/gap diagnosis → causes/problem/effects and means/objectives/ends → ToC with mechanisms, assumptions, alternatives and tests → logical framework/SMART/RACER → award criteria/choice/ID/test/question/instance matrix → response plans with verified cap. Optional charts; tables/text must allow checking of connections. A priority does not prove need; download/participation does not prove outcome; a hash does not prove causal efficacy.
