## Routine interna adattata

1. Define the desired lasting change with real targets/partners and documented needs.
2. Trace outcomes, outputs, activities and resources; review each mechanism, assumption and contributing factor moving forward.
3. Clarify who will use the outputs and what practice/skills will change; distinguish contribution to the impact from attribution to the project alone.
4. Scegliere indicatori di realizzazione e outcome, baseline o unknown, target motivato, metodo, tempo e owner; raccogliere segnali precoci e definire correzioni.
5. Select only the elements relevant to the real question, linking verified criteria and evidence in the versioned plans.

The `project-model-v2` model retains `results` for outcomes. Deliverables and milestones remain distinct: the tool's terminology table does not modify the schema/ID. Maintain connections and decisions in the plan, without a second budget/calendar.
