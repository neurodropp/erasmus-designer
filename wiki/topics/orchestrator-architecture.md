# Orchestrator, dossiers and phase validators

The system reuses sources/revisions in raw/sources, routes/profiles/checklists in config, capabilities in skills and memory maintained in LWC. Each application lives in projects/NAME--TYPE--YEAR with common identity and data, distinct responses and attachments. The full path and mandates are in [architecture](../../docs/architecture.md) and [protocol](../../docs/orchestrator-protocol.md); they are methods authorized by the user, not rules of the announcement.

## Orchestratore

The coordinator manages briefs, route, sources, common data, dependencies, versions, unique outputs and retry budgets. Retrieve details for action/question/attachment without loading entire corpus. Producers work on limited tasks; the validator's judgment remains separate. Four total agents, maximum three competing specialists.

## Each step ends with a new validator

A new spawn fork none receives frozen products/evidence/criteria and versioned dependencies; not the producers' conversation or a desired outcome. Observed identity distinct from all production and from any previous validator of the dossier. Repair/QA receive original findings, current log, and before/after baseline, with no grades to hit. Contract and syntax are in the package outline.

Accept allows step dependent. Revise/blocked require corrections or data; each attempt has another validator. Changes to approved products require invalidation of the first affected phase; Previous gates remain historic and superseded. Receipts verify hash/structure and tool observations; they do not attest to hidden runtime isolation or factual merit.

Version 0.2.1 prevents jumps to phases never reached and dispatch of outdated packets. Only active accepted products bind subsequent work; blocked/revise products may change while their snapshots remain historical. Repair passes the previous review without resetting the counters. Spawn observations have a verified historical copy.

Before freezing the gate, check path components and integrity of original→manifest→derivative→index. The dossier and package use the same general declared dependencies as the route, including relevant priorities, glossary, and standards. The `project-model-v2` model maintains tasks, deliverables/versions and milestones; application and attachments derive from those identities. Valid profiles and question/instance contexts determine the limits of the texts. They are local controls, distinct from substantive compliance.
