# Conflicts between sources and versions

Each conflict maintains rule, source/version, scope, evidence, and solution or open question. An update does not erase history. The program guide defines the conditions; the expert guide clarifies how to evaluate them and does not introduce a different requirement. The Italian documents only integrate the areas of competence.

The main verified case is the inconsistency of the expert guide on the KA220 management budget: the operational denominator is the project total.

## KA220: denominator of the management budget

The [2026 program guide, PDF p.243](../../sources/programme-2026/pages/page-0243.md) limits the management WP to 20% of the project budget. The [expert guide p.49](../../sources/expert-2026/pages/page-0049.md) indicates 20% of the required budget; [p.70](../../sources/expert-2026/pages/page-0070.md) uses a formulation relating to the budget of the other WPs. The [lump sum manual 2026, PDF p.20](../../sources/lump-sum-handbook-2026/pages/page-0020.md) confirms the total denominator.

**Result:** for KA220 use a maximum of 20% of the total, keeping the inconsistent formulation as an expert guide anomaly. Do not calculate 20% of the other WPs. This rule does not transfer to KA210: there the management reference is flexible, as explained in the [[buone-pratiche-application]] research.
