# Erasmus+ Design Dossier

Internal model to be filled in with real data, proportionate to the route and actual fields; It is not a new form nor a required attachment. Apply [the design method](../../docs/design-methodology.md) and its hierarchy of sources: relevant guide/call/form/award criteria, Handbook2026 for Partnerships for Cooperation NA, Impacttool KA1 or KA2 as methodological tools. Do not transfer Erasmus+ rules to ESC. Read [[buone-pratiche-application]] and [[priorita-italiane-2026]] when applicable.

Store as `design/design-plan-vN.md` in phase `intervention-design` (`design` compact), path relative to the file; pass the explicit version as a step-product to the gate and response plans. Tables and explanations refer to the `project.json` IDs/data, without introducing new numerical registers. Optional charts; diagnosis, causes/means-ends and ToC must be observable. An accepted product cannot be rewritten: new version and invalidation of the proprietary phase.

## Identity and route

Record provisional title, year/round, action/sector, coordinator and countries, relevant agency/call, duration, target group, contribution requested and separate overall cost. Highlight the verified requirements, those to be verified and the sources. Acquire the form before producing a final version adhering to the fields.

## Participatory diagnosis and gap analysis

Record who actually contributed, with method, date, context, evidence, limits and disagreements; distinguish observed participation from planned consultations. If the target's voice is missing, keep the diagnosis open and prepare authentic collection, without inventing answers or contacting third parties without authorization. For KA152 document young people's choices and tasks in planning, preparation, implementation and follow-up and learning needs.

| Need/ID | Group and context | Current situation/evidence | Desired situation | Gap compared with existing responses | Participation observed or to be collected | Primary/secondary/possible effect and reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model Reference | Real target | Source, date and limits; unknown if missing | Change to be validated | Services/interventions compared | Provenance or explicit plan | Benefit, equity, feasibility |

Program priorities are not tests of need; European data and local baselines remain distinct.

## Problem tree → objective tree and strategy choice

| Causes → problem → effects | Evidence/hypotheses of connections | Means → objectives → ends | Related needs/goals ID | Control/influence/external factor | Branch chosen/excluded and reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verifiable conditions | Test or test needed | Desired conditions to review | Canonical IDs available | Real capacity of the partnership | Relevant alternatives compared |

Do not formulate the problem merely as the absence of the preferred solution. Reversing a sentence does not demonstrate causality: explain the connection and how it can be addressed. The choice preserves a relevant alternative, decision criteria and open dependencies.

## Theory of Change

| Resources/activities → output → outcome → contribution to impact | Canonical IDs | Mechanism: who uses what and which practice changes | Assumption/external factor | Evidence or test, time and owner | Response if it does not hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Selected chain | Reference to the model | Verifiable Explanation | Status and limit | Proportionate collection/testing | Revision or alternative |

Design backwards from lasting change and test the chain forward. Distinguish product/service output, outcome (`results`) and broader impact; participation, diffusion and satisfaction do not automatically prove changes. Specify relevant contributing factors and undesirable effects, adoption path and attribution limits.

## Activities, results and indicators

| Objective/ID | Relevant Activities/WPs/ID | Output/ID | Outcome/ID | Indicator/ID and definition | Baselines | Motivated target | Method/data | Time/owner | Assumptions/risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference to the model | Structure of the action | Product/service | Observable change | Unit, denominator or rubric | Given or unknown/plan | Reference to the base in the model | Tool/Test | Stage and real partner | Reference to the ToC/register |

Record SMART assessments for objectives and RACER assessments for indicators, with evidence/gaps: Relevant, Accepted, Credible, Easy, Robust. “Accepted” requires actual agreement on the definition, method and responsibility; otherwise it remains to be confirmed. Check that measurement is at the right level, the burden is proportionate, and the method is robust. An unknown baseline, a verified zero, and a justified NA are different; do not invent percentages to make the goal measurable.

## Award criteria and moving on to the answers

| Official criterion/subpoint and source/version/scope | Supported choice and ID | Test/locator | Relevant question/instance | Official or interpretative mapping | Coverage/gap and owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Actual route grid | Need, goal, partner, activity, outcome or impact | Evidence observed | Exact field, when known | Mapping provenance | Concrete support or necessary intervention |

Connect criteria across sections as well; one choice can support multiple criteria, and one criterion can apply to multiple fields. There is no official sub-scoring by item/question. For an administrative field, use a justified evaluative NA while preserving the form's requirements. The [answer contract](response-contract.md) governs `application/answer-plan-vN.md`, specific item selection, and counts per question/instance.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real data | Evidence | Responsibility | Check | Source/date | Complementarity | Decisions and conflicts |

Estimate costs starting from activities, using the action regime. For KA220 check management maximum 20% of the total and appropriate lump sum; for KA210 use simplified activity structure. For KA1 apply categories, units and rates of your action and national provisions. See [[conflitti-fonti]].

## Inclusion, quality and impact

Describe concrete barriers and supports, motivated green and digital choices, EU participation and values, relevant protection and safety, monitoring and risks with managers. For outcomes, plan for use and adoption, recognition of learning, communication, access/licensing, and continuity beyond the grant. Each commitment is confirmed or explicitly conditional.

## Review and handover

Freeze the plan, model and dependencies before independent critique. Use [[revisione-application]] to record findings and corrections. State which checks were carried out, which were not, and any gaps; unsupported needs, causal leaps, incompatible resources or unjustified targets require further research/design. Unresolved forms, limits or mandatory data keep the application in draft form. Store summary decisions and evidence, without chain of thought. Responsibility for the content remains with the applicants/partners, as stated in the 2026 Programme Guide, p.428.
