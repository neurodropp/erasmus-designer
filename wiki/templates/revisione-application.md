# Erasmus+ Application Review

Use in conjunction with [Documented Good Practices](../research/buone-pratiche-application.md). This template organizes a professional review; it is not a new official rubric and does not guarantee the Agency's score. The criteria, caps and thresholds must be imported from the relevant action and annuity, not from the template.

## 5. Budget, implementation and safeguarding

- Correct regime for each category? Have estimated quantities/costs been verified? Is the allocation justified by tasks? Is co-financing applicable and realistic?
- KA220: WP management within 20% of the **total** and WP implementation defined; maximum four WP implementation is recommendation. KA210: management embedded in activities; reference 20% flexible, not automatic roof.
- Deliverable quality/acceptance, milestones, monitoring and corrective actions present? Risks with prevention, response and managers?
- Applicable safety, insurance and minor clearances verified? Concretely sustainable participant supports?

Classify each observation R/V/M/C. Sources: [P 2026, pp.11,243,249–250](<../../raw/pdf/programme-guide-2026-en.pdf>), [E 2026, pp.49,61,70–71](<../../raw/pdf/expert-guide-2026-v1.pdf>). Keep the note on the inconsistency of E p.70 described in the research; apply the Program Guide.
