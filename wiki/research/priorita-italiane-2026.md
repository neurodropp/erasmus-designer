# Italian priorities Erasmus+ 2026 by sector and action

The recovery key must include **year, sector, action, country of the coordinator and body receiving the application**. A national priority is a European priority considered particularly relevant in the Italian context: it is not a set of objectives alternative to the European ones. This is clarified by [INAPP FAQ on partnerships, 2026 edition, p.7](https://www.erasmusplus.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/FAQ-KA2-2026-03_02_2026-DEF.pdf).

## INDIRE: SCH and ADU

All four horizontal areas are supported: inclusion and diversity; digital learning and access; environmental education and sustainability; democratic participation. The provision does not define a ranking between these areas.

For KA210/KA220, with the same total score, the order of the criteria is: relevance, impact, development/implementation, partnership/cooperation. If parity persists, priority will be given to new applicants or less experienced organizations according to the glossary of the Guide. These are rules for ex aequo, not additional points to be invented.

For KA122/KA120 consult pp.4 and 7 separately. To distinguish INDIRE and VET mobility, consult the notes on pp.2 and 5.

Source: [National provisions SCH/ADU 2026, pp.2, 4, 5, 7–8](https://www.erasmusplus.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Erasmus-Disposizioni-nazionali-SCH_ADU-2026.pdf). PDF pages numbered from 1; page 8 also verified via screenshot.

## INDIRE: higher education

For KA220-HED the PDF HE supports inclusion/diversity, digital, environmental sustainability and civic participation. In ex aequo the order is relevance, impact, elaboration/implementation. This source does not add the partnership criterion nor the precedence for new applicants reported in the SCH/ADU document: keep the rule separately.

Source: [HE Provisions 2026, version 2 of May, p.9](https://www.erasmusplus.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Erasmus-Disposizioni-nazionali-HE_2026_v-2.pdf). Text and numbering verified via PDF; screenshot not obtained due to service cache error. It is a verified official release, not a claim that no subsequent updates may be released.

## INAPP: vocational education and training

Four horizontal areas: inclusion of people with fewer opportunities; sustainability; digital capabilities, including AI and assistive technologies; European citizenship, preparation and resilience.

The three VET areas selected are:

1. Paths adapted to the labor market and fundamental skills, with learning on the job.
2. Flexibility: modules, recognition of informal/non-formal learning and continuing adult education.
3. Innovation and quality: skills ecosystems, EQF 5–8, applied research, SME services, trainer development and EQAVET.

Source: [VET Priorities 2026, pp.1–2](https://www.erasmusplus.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Priorita_nazionali_Erasmus_VET_2026_24_02_26.pdf); page 2 also verified via screenshot. It does not contain a national rule for ex aequo: do not deduce one, nor import the INDIRE one.

For the connection to the two types of partnership see the [FAQ INAPP 2026, p.7](https://www.erasmusplus.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/FAQ-KA2-2026-03_02_2026-DEF.pdf) and the [official resources for Infodays KA210/KA220 2026](https://www.erasmusplus.it/formazione/risorse-e-strumenti/).

## AIG: Youth

The February 26 communication selects two areas for KA220-YOU from the March 5 deadline:

- Quality, innovation and recognition of socio-educational work and non-formal/informal learning: EYWA, Bonn Declaration and Growing Youth Work.
- Employability through fundamental and transversal skills, also EntreComp: Youth@Work and Union of Skills; attention to NEET, fewer opportunities, exclusion and migratory background.

The horizontal and sectoral priorities of the Guide remain applicable. National areas are additional elements for financing projects that achieve the required quality; the news does not define automatic points, guarantee of financing or a specific criterion for ex aequo.

Source: [AIG, European priorities in the national context for KA220, 26 February 2026](https://agenziagioventu.gov.it/priorita-europee-nel-contesto-nazionale-rilevanti-nel-processo-di-valutazione-dei-partenariati-di-cooperazione-ka220/). HTML source, so no PDF page number.

## Decision for the second brain

These are knowledge compilation rules proposed for the assistant, not instructions from the sources:

1. Create separate records for SCH, ADU, HED, VET, YOU and Sport, indicating the action covered and the initial date of application, if any.
2. Retain `confirmed` for explicit content and scope; `interpretation` for links from multiple sources; `unverified` when an applicable document is missing. Don't use `provisional` to detract from already official 2026 PDFs.
3. Separate European priorities, European priorities of national importance, eligibility, economic rules and rules for ex aequo. The recovery of a priority does not replace the recovery of the quality criteria of the action.
4. In the application form, link each priority chosen to the documented need, recipients, activities, results and indicators. Thematic label alone does not prove relevance.
5. Repeat the check on the official website before applying: the pages can mix the 2025 archive and the 2026 Call. An updated version must replace the applicable one, preserving the history, without reclassifying a 2025 document as 2026.

## Limitations of the verification

The check identifies the official 2026 provisions available and referenced above. It does not prove the absolute absence of further updates. No national AIG preference for KA210-YOU or KA182, nor a national VET rule for ex aequo, has been established by the sources verified here. In such cases the assistant must recall the Guide and the call for action and report the absence of a specific national confirmation.
