# Exploitation, institutional adoption, and sustainability

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **follow-up-plan** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `follow-up-plan` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Read the ToC and adoption pathway in the [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md), selecting the relevant Impacttool KA1/KA2 for Erasmus+. **Inputs:** model, versioned design-plan, outputs/outcomes/impacts, observed commitments, and resources available after the grant. **Steps:** start from the lasting change and trace who will use/adopt which result, through which activities, conditions, and resources; assess plausibility and external assumptions forward. Link owners, reasoned SMART targets, milestones, RACER indicators, and evidence of use to relevant impact/sustainability criteria, including cross-cutting criteria.

**Output:** reuse/adoption/continuity and monitoring plan, linked to IDs and the logical framework, with limitations and a relevant alternative. **Checks:** dissemination ≠ use ≠ change; distinguish actual commitments, conditions, and aspirations. Do not attribute impact to the project alone or turn Impacttool time horizons into time-bound obligations. Handbook2026 applies only to its own KA2 NA; for KA152, include young people's role in follow-up and use of learning. Do not reuse CONNECTED/adulti55+ from the model as real targets.

**Stop:** adoption without an actor/capacity/evidence, or with unconfirmed resources, remains conditional; request additions from the coordinator without creating new numbers or commitments in the annex alone.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
