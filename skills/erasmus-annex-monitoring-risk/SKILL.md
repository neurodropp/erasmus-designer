# Monitoring plan, quality gates, and risk register

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **monitoring-risk-plan** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `monitoring-risk-plan` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Read the ToC assumptions and logical framework in the [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md). **Inputs:** model/projections, versioned design-plan, KPIs, monitoring resources, and action context. **Steps:** link KPIs, baselines, data collection, frequency, and owners to outputs/outcomes and the decisive ToC assumptions. For each relevant risk, define probability/impact, warning signal, mitigation, contingency, and review; plan a test of the causal link and a response if it does not hold. Link checks to SMART objectives, RACER indicators, and official criteria supported by management quality, without treating criteria as universal.

**Output:** plan and register with canonical IDs, checkpoints, evidence, and adaptation decisions. **Checks:** calculate any risk scores from their drivers, without hardcoded numbers; distinguish risks to change/adoption from mere production delays. Practical data collection and agreed responsibilities require a factual basis; include safeguarding/data protection only where supported by relevant context and sources. Quality gates for the funded project remain distinct from engine gates.

**Stop:** a decisive assumption without a test, owner, or resource remains open and must go to the coordinator; do not introduce KPIs, targets, or confirmed mitigations absent from the model. Do not transfer the Handbook regime to KA1/ESC.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
