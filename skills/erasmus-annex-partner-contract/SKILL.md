# Partner agreement structure

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **partner-contract** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `partner-contract` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Prepare a draft tailored to the parties, their authority, the grant agreement, and confirmed decisions: roles, governance, allocations, payments, deliverables/acceptance, reporting, ownership/use of results, data, changes, disputes, and exit. Do not invent law, jurisdiction, penalties, or grant obligations. Keep the status draft-for-review until the rules and parties are verified; ready-to-sign requires evidence of legal review. Drafting does not authorize signing or submission.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
