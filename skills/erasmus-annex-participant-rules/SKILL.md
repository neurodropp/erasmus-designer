# Reimbursement process and code of conduct

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **participant-rules-code** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `participant-rules-code` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Parameterize the agreement/reimbursement rules and code of conduct by programme, action, country, age, and funding mechanism. Distinguish participant support, organizational support, and reimbursements; clarify amounts/sources, procedures, expenses/documents/deadlines to be confirmed, inclusion, safeguarding, contacts and reporting channels, conduct, and proportionate handling of issues. Do not invent deductions, penalties, or waivers of rights. Keep the status draft-for-review; ready-to-sign requires legal review and actual data.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
