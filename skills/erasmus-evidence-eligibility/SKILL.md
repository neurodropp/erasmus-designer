# Erasmus+ evidence and eligibility

Read the [evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md). First establish the year, action, sector, coordinator, and territory; retrieve the dossier and search for specific requirements. Verify the original passage and its version before translating it into a rule.

If the mandate is Phase 0 and the call has not yet been selected, translate the problem, target group, organization's role, essential activities, countries, and scale into research requirements. Build a shortlist from current official sources under `config/phase-zero.json`, including at least one credible alternative when one exists. For each candidate, specify the programme, year/round, action/sector, managing body, role, eligible target groups and activities, duration, funding model, any tiers, priorities/award fit, incompatibilities, and verification date. Do not assign a route based on thematic similarity or create the project file before the choice is verified.

Produce a matrix with requirement, scope, cited evidence, project data, outcome `soddisfatto / non soddisfatto / da verificare`, and required action. Cover only relevant requirements: eligible organisations and roles, partnership composition, countries, target groups, duration, activities, deadline, application procedure, any limits, and funding conditions. Missing data remain to be verified; do not replace them with a plausible choice.

For Italian priorities, identify the competent agency from current official sources and distinguish programme priorities, national/sectoral priorities, and general informational advice. An expert guide describes how to evaluate; it does not automatically introduce a new eligibility requirement. A source from a previous call is historical context unless explicitly confirmed as current.

Also distinguish call objectives/priorities, applicable Erasmus+ priorities, contextual EU frameworks, and complementarity with other programmes. European Youth Goals, EU strategies, and priorities of other programmes may support coherence and added value; they become a requirement or criterion only when incorporated by the applicable source. Do not transfer eligibility, deadlines, budgets, or scoring grids across programmes.

Deliver an evidence register, blocking requirements, unresolved issues, and research coverage. Report the sections searched when a provision was not found. Suggest the next concrete check without certifying eligibility when mandatory data are missing or sources conflict.

Also deliver the route's criteria and subpoints with locators, official weights only, and scopes verified for design and response planning. The [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md) links them to choices and questions; editorial field mapping remains distinct from a correspondence attested by the form. Handbook2026 applies to the relevant Partnerships for Cooperation NA; Impact Tool KA1/KA2 are methodological aids. Do not derive character limits, new Italian conditions, or requirements for ESC/EACEA from them.

For idea validation, retrieve the profile with `scripts/award_criteria.py catalog/show` and [complete the specific checklist](../../docs/award-idea-checklists.md). Read the selected rubric, conditions, and conditional subpoints in full; confirm the variant/role without a default. Distinguish evidence, plausibility, and research still needed; clarify which gap changes the idea and which requires later evidence. Accreditation or allocation without a new rubric must not be assigned invented scored criteria.
