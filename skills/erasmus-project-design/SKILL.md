# Erasmus+ and ESC project design

Read the [evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md), eligibility matrix, [Phase 0 — Idea Lab](../../docs/phase-zero-idea-lab.md), and [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md). Its hierarchies and scope distinctions apply even when an annex or field addresses only part of the chain. For KA2 Partnerships for Cooperation managed by an NA, use the relevant Handbook2026; to formulate impact, choose Impacttool KA1 or KA2. Do not derive Italian rules, form limits, or WP requirements for other actions from these sources.

**Input:** project-file memory, brief, Phase 0 decision contract if one exists, diagnosis/research evidence, route/award criteria/form, model, and confirmed partner capacities. If requirements are unavailable, retrieve them before fixing the architecture and partnership. Phase 0 with `REFINE`, `PIVOT`, or `STOP` does not authorize proceeding as if the route were validated.

**Steps:** if the mandate concerns an initial idea, conduct divergent brainstorming and then apply the Phase 0 contract: route discovery/verification, priorities at multiple levels, primary/secondary target groups, actual scale or tier, preliminary chain, and recommendation `PROCEED|REFINE|PIVOT|STOP`. Distinguish route priorities from European Youth Goals and other contextual frameworks; do not assign an official score. In subsequent design, verify the participatory diagnosis and gap; link causes/problem/effects and critically transform them into means/objectives/ends. Select the addressable branch, compare a relevant alternative, and build a ToC with use mechanisms, assumptions, external factors, and decisive tests. Derive the logical framework, SMART objectives, and RACER indicators, with evidence or gaps. Map official criteria across needs, objectives, partners, activities, results, and impact; distinguish interpretations from official mappings. Use common IDs, proposing updates to `project.json` to the coordinator.

**Output:** for Phase 0, `phase-zero-decision.md` compliant with the [contract](../../wiki/templates/phase-zero-decision-contract.md), in the path assigned by the workflow, with sources, alternatives, recommendation, and observed choice kept distinct; bind each choice to the exact idea version, route, and source snapshot, and do not invent a project file before establishing the route. For design, `design/design-plan-vN.md`, a version of the `intervention-design` product (`design` compact), with diagnosis, including textual trees, ToC, logical framework, award-coverage, alternative/choice, and open dependencies. Numbers and IDs refer to the model; the plan explains without creating a second source of truth. Explicitly deliver it as a step-product with the relevant dependencies.

**Checks:** output ≠ outcome (`results`) ≠ impact; no automatic attribution. Justify European added value, owner, and complementarity with demonstrated capacity; make inclusion, green, digital, and participation operational where relevant. Adapt resources/WP to the action: KA210 by activity, KA1 without imposing a KA220 architecture. For KA152, verify young people's co-design and tasks across all phases and learning.

**Stop:** in Phase 0, incompatible applicant/target/activities, an unverified route, an unresolved decisive requirement, or an unsustainable scale prevents `PROCEED`; distinguish a gap from a confirmed hard blocker and consider `REFINE` or `PIVOT` before `STOP`. In design, an unsupported central need, causal leap, unsupported target group, or unresolved requirement keeps the product conditional and requires research/revision. Charts are optional; analysis and links must be observable. A change to an accepted product requires a new version and invalidation by the owning phase.

For ESC, retrieve the exact route using [esc-evidence-eligibility](../esc-evidence-eligibility/SKILL.md): criteria, requirements, funding, and role remain programme-specific. Do not transfer Erasmus+ rates or scoring grids.
