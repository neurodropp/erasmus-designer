# Erasmus+ and ESC quality and impact

Read [the evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and [the design method](../../docs/design-methodology.md). Use frozen versions of the model, design-plan, answer-plan, application form, evidence, annexes, budget, and findings. A methodological checklist does not resolve eligibility or independently verify causality.

**Steps:** check the participatory diagnosis/gap and the link causes→objectives→activities→outputs→outcomes→impact; locate gaps, untested decisive assumptions, and ignored relevant alternatives. Check the logical framework, SMART/RACER outcomes with evidence, baseline/targets, measurement burden, owner, timing, and adoption pathway. Compare award-coverage and the criterion/question/instance matrix against the actual design and text, including cross-cutting links; interpretive mappings and administrative NAs must be justified, without invented subscores.

**Output:** an impact plan or QA report as required by the mandate, locating each defect, evidence, consequence, and correction; separate checks completed, not executable, and open.

**Checks:** outputs, dissemination, use, and outcomes are not synonyms; state contribution and attribution limits. Dissemination has relevant audiences/channels/timing/owners and measures; sustainability has ownership, resources, and evidence of use. Agreements, capacity, and adoption cannot be inferred from promises. Handbook2026 applies to its own KA2 NA scope; Impacttool KA1/KA2 supports the method without imposing Italian rules/WP/form limits. For KA152, verify young people's tasks and choices in every phase and learning.

Before delivery, check: mandatory questions and annexes required by the source/form; consistent numbers and roles across sections; compatible calendar and budget; navigable references; known field limits; no placeholders or unsupported factual claims. Check accessibility and other cross-cutting aspects as relevant to recipients and criteria, not as decorative lists.

**Stop:** a substantive gap requires design/model correction and dependent verification, not rewriting alone. The status `ready for human review` requires evidence of checks and complete required data; it does not mean consent or submission. Accepted plans are not rewritten: create a new version and invalidate from the first affected phase. QA does not replace the new independent gate.

Deliver an impact plan or QA report as assigned, plus reusable lessons confirmed with sources and scope. Propose capturing them in curated memory through the coordinator; do not promote a project hypothesis to a programme rule.

For ESC, retrieve the exact route with [esc-evidence-eligibility](../esc-evidence-eligibility/SKILL.md): criteria, requirements, funding, and roles are programme-specific. Do not transfer Erasmus+ rates or scoring grids.
