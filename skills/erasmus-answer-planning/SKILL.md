# Response planning

Read the [response contract](../../wiki/templates/response-contract.md), [evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md), and award criteria/response section of the [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md). For the user's KA220 DOCX, consult only the relevant fields in the [playbook](../../wiki/templates/ka220-question-playbook.md) and the profile: template2025 is unofficial, and other forms have their own profiles.

**Input:** form/route and verified limits for each question/instance, the relevant `design/design-plan-vN.md`, model/evidence, and award-coverage. Identify year/round, programme, action, and conditional contexts.

**Steps:** for each question/instance pair, select the elements required from the diagnosis/gap, trees, ToC, and logical framework; link them to claims, IDs, evidence, and relevant official criteria, including cross-cutting criteria. Retain the prompt/locator, limit/evidence, gaps, specific structure, and editorial word allocations within the maximum. An administrative field has a reasoned evaluative NA and retains form requirements. Distinguish official from interpretive mapping; do not invent subscores or methodological caps.

**Output:** `application/answer-plan-vN.md`, the versioned `answer-outlines` product in the integrated process (`draft` in compact, before drafting and frozen with the texts), containing a criterion/question/instance matrix, specific plans, evidence, and issues/alternatives; any `answer-outlines.md` is a presentation of the same plan. Explicitly pass it as a step-product to the gate and writer together with the approved design.

**Checks:** field completeness, design support for the criteria, distinct output/outcome/impact, relevant SMART/RACER, and consistency with the model/annexes. Preserve identity and per-instance caps; use `null` when the maximum is unknown. Do not put the entire framework in every response. Retain checklists and concise rationales, without chain of thought.

**Stop:** a substantive defect returns to design; unverified limits/forms keep the plan in draft. The writer applies `scripts/application.py` to every version. Changes to accepted plans require a new version and invalidation by the owning phase.
