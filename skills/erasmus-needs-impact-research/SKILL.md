# Needs and impact research

Read the [contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md), especially diagnosis, trees, and ToC. Handbook2026 and Impacttool are references with different scopes: apply the methodology's hierarchy, without transferring their rules to ESC or treating a tool as the source of form limits.

**Input:** actual problem/target group, memory, partner context, and research questions; verified award criteria guide which claims to support but do not constitute evidence of need.

**Steps:** formulate questions and criteria for retaining/narrowing/rejecting the idea; triangulate located primary sources and genuine participation by the target group/partners. Compare the current and desired situations and existing interventions in a gap analysis; prioritize primary/secondary needs and possible effects. Sketch a problem tree and objective tree with proven/hypothesized links, an addressable branch, and alternatives. Verify mechanisms and evidence for the proposed ToC, assumptions, competing factors, and unintended effects; consider only relevant impact levels.

In Phase 0, apply `config/phase-zero.json`: explore alternative formulations before converging, distinguish primary and secondary target groups, and build the preliminary chain needs→problems→solutions→activities→output→results→impact. For each step, record the claim, target, mechanism, evidence status, assumption, risk/external factor, and next test. Examine participants, organisations, communities, the system/sector, and European level only when relevant; reach and dissemination are not impact in themselves.

**Output:** critical report, diagnosis/gap matrices, claim/source register and observed participation, alternatives/choice, and collection plan. Pass causes, candidate objectives, ToC hypotheses, and SMART/RACER foundations tied to IDs to design; propose needs/impacts/KPI for `project.json`, for the coordinator to integrate.

**Checks:** distinguish unit, territory, date, population, method, and limitations; an EU statistic is not a local baseline. A priority or funded example does not prove need, effectiveness, or likelihood of success. For KA152, collect evidence of young people's choices and learning needs. Missing consultations remain planned; do not invent responses or contact third parties without authorization.

**Stop:** insufficient evidence or absent target-group voice prevents a validated diagnosis; deliver open dependencies and conditional options. Do not invent a baseline/attribution or save chain of thought.
