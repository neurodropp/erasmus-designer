# Meeting/event pack and evidence register

Read the [shared evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md), the **meetings-evidence** blueprint, and its `config/annexes.json` record. Consult the [template catalogue](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) only where relevant.

Record meetings and decisions with purpose, activity/work package, required participants, agenda, date, owner, action items, and acceptance. Never invent past meetings or minutes. Collect only necessary evidence and manage access without exposing personal data or photographs because a template appears to require them.


Apply the [shared delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md).
