# Budget Erasmus+ ed ESC

Read the [evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md). Retrieve the financial section for the action and year before choosing formulas: unit contributions, actual costs, and lump sums are not interchangeable. Use amounts, tiers, conditions, and percentages only with an applicable citation.

In Phase 0, assess only strategic proportionality under `config/phase-zero.json`: verified funding model, tiers or rates only if actually provided, plausible activities/participants/partners/duration, capacity, a credible minimum version, and a downsizing alternative. The highest tier is not a goal; it follows from the intervention logic and resources. If the route or rules are not yet verified, record scenarios and dependencies without declaring costs eligible or producing a final budget.

Start from the activities and resources required. For each line item, document quantity, unit, assumptions, source of the amount, responsible partner, and linked activity/result. For a lump sum, justify the need and proportionality under the actual rules; do not automatically treat it as reimbursement of every estimated cost. If a tier must be selected, show the rationale and conditions.

Check totals, allocation among partners and periods, any ceilings, double counting, and consistency among the calendar, staff, participants, outputs, and application text. Verify using reproducible calculations with available local tools. Do not enter actual costs or unconfirmed commitments as final data.

Deliver the budget table and assumptions note, arithmetic check results, cited rules, and missing information. Classify amounts lacking a quote or user-provided data as estimates; distinguish a calculation error from uncertainty about financial eligibility. Withhold a compliance conclusion if the model or applicable rules are unverified.

For ESC, retrieve the exact route using [esc-evidence-eligibility](../esc-evidence-eligibility/SKILL.md): criteria, requirements, funding, and role remain programme-specific. Do not transfer Erasmus+ rates or scoring grids.
