# Reporting, evidence, and approval checklist

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **reporting-checklist** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `reporting-checklist` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Derive deadlines, owners, documents, evidence, and acceptance criteria from the current grant, call, and internal decisions. Distinguish application, implementation, and reporting; the template does not create obligations. Check dates and dependencies, including the absence of impossible dates such as 31 September.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
