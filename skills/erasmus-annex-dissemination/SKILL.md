# Dissemination plan and register

Read the [shared evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md), the **dissemination-table** blueprint, and its `config/annexes.json` record. Consult the [template catalogue](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) only where relevant.

Distinguish communication, dissemination, use, and adoption. For each audience define the action, channel, product, accessibility, owner, schedule, KPI, and evidence collection. View counts do not prove change. Reconcile products and dates with work packages, Gantt, and result registers.


Apply the [shared delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md).
