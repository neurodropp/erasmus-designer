# Gantt chart and milestones

Read the [shared evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md), the **gantt-table** blueprint, and its `config/annexes.json` record. Consult the [template catalogue](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) only where relevant.

Derive the schedule from approved activities, tasks, and milestones. Check dependencies, duration, partner workload, releases, and reviews before deliverables. Use weeks or months at the precision supported by the data; never invent exact dates from an indicative month. A schedule change reopens dependent budget, task, and dissemination checks.


Apply the [shared delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md).
