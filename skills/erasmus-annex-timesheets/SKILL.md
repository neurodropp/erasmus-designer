# Effort, timesheets, and conditional staff statements

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **timesheets-register** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `timesheets-register` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Check whether timesheets or staff statements are actually required by the grant or are only useful internally. Keep person/role, period, task/activity/WP, effort, unit, and approval consistent with the budget and workload. Do not invent hours worked or impose actual-cost reporting on a lump sum.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
