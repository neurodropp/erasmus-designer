# KPI and evidence plan

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **kpi-table** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `kpi-table` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Read the logical framework and SMART/RACER sections of the [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md). **Inputs:** KPIs/model objects, versioned design-plan, target baselines, collection resources, and observed agreements. **Steps:** associate each indicator with the level/ID actually measured; define the unit, numerator/denominator or rubric, actual/unknown baseline, reasoned target, source/method, frequency, owner, and evidence. Assess RACER: relevance to the objective, actual shared understanding of definition/method/responsibility, verifiability, practical burden, and robustness to bias/manipulation. Link measures to relevant criteria without inventing official thresholds.

**Output:** KPI table and evidence plan with RACER assessment and gaps, linked to the logical framework; no parallel numerical register. **Checks:** distinguish outputs/outcomes/impact; indicator ≠ target; visibility/participation ≠ change. Verify the SMART basis of the measured objective and attribution limits; proposed targets and unconfirmed shared commitments remain subject to confirmation. Handbook2026 Annex2 applies to its own KA2 NA; RACER is a method, not a source of new obligations for another route.

**Stop:** an unknown baseline does not become zero, and NA requires a reason; missing decisive data/agreement or causal link requires collection or redesign through the coordinator. Do not fabricate targets to fill cells.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
