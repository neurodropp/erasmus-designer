# WPs, activities, tasks, outputs, and deliverables

Read [the shared contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the **work-packages-table** blueprint in [annex-blueprints](../../wiki/templates/annex-blueprints.md). Find the `work-packages-table` record in `config/annexes.json` for columns, inputs, source refs, and checks. The [catalog](../../wiki/templates/annex-template-catalog.md) locates the original worksheets; read only the parts relevant to the mandate.

Read the ToC, logical framework, and award-coverage sections of the [design methodology](../../docs/design-methodology.md). **Inputs:** model and verified projections, versioned design-plan, route/regime, and annex mandate. **Steps:** translate the selected ToC branch into activities and, where relevant, WPs/tasks/deliverables with canonical IDs; link SMART objectives, outputs, outcomes, resources, reasoned owners, dependencies, acceptance, and deadlines. Check the activity→output→use/outcome mechanism and relevant criteria, including cross-cutting criteria, without producing a new numerical design.

**Output:** requested table with references to the logical framework, relevant criteria, and assumptions/risks. **Checks:** use WPs proportionate to the route; KA210 retains an activity-based structure, and KA1/ESC do not automatically receive KA220 WPs. Handbook2026 applies only to its own KA2 NA. Products and changes are distinct; each delivery has an owner and acceptance criteria, without inventing acceptance that has already occurred. For KA152, make young people's activities/tasks and learning visible.

**Stop:** a weak link or discrepancy with the model/projections requires a proposal to the coordinator and review of the owning phase; do not patch it with alternative tasks, targets, or calendars in the workbook.


Apply the [common delivery contract](../erasmus-designer/references/annex-delivery-contract.md) for shared data, manifest, checks, and output format.
