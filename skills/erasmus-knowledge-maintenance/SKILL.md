# Source and form maintenance

Use this skill to update the shared corpus, handle a new call, or process a corrigendum. For the eligibility of an individual application, use the relevant evidence-eligibility skill; for its form alone, follow [form-intake](../../docs/form-intake.md). Maintenance does not authorize submitting an application, signing, contacting third parties, new logins, or recurring automation.

Identify the engine root according to [path-conventions](../../docs/path-conventions.md), then read [the executable procedure](../../docs/knowledge-maintenance.md), `config/knowledge-maintenance.json`, `config/actions.json`, and the relevant records in `config/sources.json`. Retrieve memory with `scripts/lwc search` and `page show`; compare originals through `brain.py` without treating a source-map as regulatory analysis. Check versions and corrigenda online against official sources. Catalog URLs are entry points to revisit, not a list that is always up to date.

The [erasmus-designer](../erasmus-designer/SKILL.md) coordinator manages updates involving regulations, forms, and the engine. Delegate only concrete, independent tasks, specifying the skill to read, assets/programmes/years, and exclusive paths: Erasmus+ evidence-eligibility, ESC evidence-eligibility, form schemas, and engine integration. Maximum three specialists plus the coordinator; NA guides do not replace EACEA documents/grids.

For each asset, retain programme, year, action, sector, round, language, agency, jurisdiction, and scope. Distinguish programme guides, expert guides, corrigenda, EU/NA/EACEA pages, samples, and actual forms. A card's date may precede replacement of its PDF: record separately the document header/version, stated publication date, acquisition, and actually measured remote hash. Do not change the year in a URL to assume the next source exists.

Use `scripts/knowledge_maintenance.py` for inventory, capture/stage, discover, check, diff, report, and export. Hash-based revisions remain **staged** outside catalogs and active originals. Deterministic checks are technical evidence, not an independent review. Public reading and unauthenticated capture may proceed under the already authorized mandate; a private export stays in the selected file. Never write directly to `.lwc/` or databases.

For forms, acquire an export/print and, where needed, localized evidence from the already authorized platform. Scan all relevant sections and conditional paths: exact text/help, type, required status, limits/counters and counting rule, options, tables, repetitions per partner/WP/activity/role, conditions, uploads, and award mappings. Mark unobservable branches as unverified; do not enter data, costs, or answers to reveal fields without a specific mandate. Samples may omit limits, conditionals, and instances; do not claim complete coverage from a printed PDF.

Produce a profile that conforms to `form_profile.validate_profile`; record questions and rich fields without losing their metadata. Extraction/enumeration IDs and IDs derived from labels are not official platform IDs. A stable namespace requires registry evidence; if it changes, prepare a one-to-one crosswalk with evidence of identity/section/context. Similar labels are ambiguous suggestions. Compare the complete JSON, including instances/conditions/limits and associated grids; retain added/removed/changed/ambiguous. A missing, zero, or reduced limit requires resolution; do not truncate or transfer answers automatically.

Carry the update through to promotion when sources and evidence are sufficient:

1. Freeze the maintenance package: originals/manifest, previous versions, diff, complete route requirements matrix, conflicts, dependency list, and plan for affected files. Record actual limitations/weaknesses, a relevant alternative, and an evidence-based decision.
2. The coordinator assigns integration in an isolated candidate engine. Explicitly update catalogs and years, routing/page scopes from actual physical pages, action criteria and thresholds, national notes, profiles/playbooks, checklists, and retrieval tests. For a new year, genuinely extend and test `brain.py`, `workflow.py`, action modes, and other contracts with year guards; a JSON change does not make the call supported. Preserve sources and routing for previous years. Do not blindly recompile existing summaries with `sync_lwc.py`.
3. Build candidate extractions from verified originals; run `brain.py check`, retrieval specific to programmes/actions/years, profile validation, limit/instance tests, gates, and workflow. Prepare a candidate release using the existing process, without altering previous releases and receipts. Adapt the allowlist/capability adapter only with evidence and tests: currently the indexed engine and adapter recognize 2026; 2027 remains staged.
4. Start a **new** `fork_turns="none"` agent, distinct from producers and all previous validators, using [erasmus-stage-validator](../erasmus-stage-validator/SKILL.md), the frozen package, and an exclusive report path. Do not suggest an outcome. For application phases use `stage_gate.py` and the fresh-step-v1 protocol; for an engine update use the relevant package/release gate and state any contract differences, without inventing an application workflow. Every completed repair/review/QA requires another fresh reviewer.
5. After acceptance, publish shared memory through the **LWC CLI**: checkpoint, isolated changeset, source/ingest and pages with citations, lint/retrieval before and after, explicit commit, and receipt. The operating guide contains the actual commands and documented non-atomic recovery. Promote the catalog/engine/release only against accepted hashes; any change reopens verification. Do not present staging, source add, or a simple rebuild as complete publication.
6. Bind the form only to selected files, with the profile and localized evidence copied to the respective inbox/application, a new independent check, and `workflow.py bind-form`. A regulatory change invalidates the first dependent production phase; a replaced form reopens answer-outlines/draft and dependencies according to the process. Preserve registry IDs, original answers, evidence, attempt budget, and superseded history. Do not bulk-migrate applications or years.

If a guide, form, or evidence for a limit is missing, continue independent work and leave that specific update open. Deliver what is verified and the necessary data, without fabricating a blocked/accept status or a spawn. If the review requests changes, freeze the findings baseline and new before/after evidence, then repeat with a new reviewer.

For rollback, explicitly select the previous accepted revision, retain the candidate, diff, report, and receipts, restore catalog/routing/form against its hashes, and invalidate affected dependencies. Before commit, discard the changeset; afterward, use rollback of the exact ID only if the CLI confirms there have been no subsequent live writes. Restoring from a checkpoint is a separate operation that must be justified and verified. Do not delete revisions or rewrite signatures/history to make rollback appear continuous.

Deliver the inventory and manifest, sources consulted, check results, complete diff and any crosswalk, independent report/actual receipts, promoted changes, status of selected files, and unresolved issues. Distinguish collected, verified, accepted, published, and active.
