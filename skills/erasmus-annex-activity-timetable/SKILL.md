# Educational activity timetable

Read the [shared evidence contract](../erasmus-designer/references/evidence-contract.md) and the `ka1-activity-timetable` record in `config/annexes.json`. The 2026 Programme Guide, physical page 157, requires a timetable for each planned KA152 exchange and preparatory visit. Verify the actual form, round, and upload format. For other actions, obtain the applicable source before calling an annex mandatory.

Use the activities, dates, learning objectives, and partners from the common model. Structure days and sessions around non-formal methods, realistic timing, ownership/facilitation, the concrete role of young people, and the origin of their proposals. A completed table does not prove co-design: request evidence of choices and contributions and leave missing facts unconfirmed. Never invent dates or participants; use indicative days only when the design maturity permits it.

Check duration, travel, venue, inclusion, breaks, reflection/Youthpass, and safeguarding where relevant. Reconcile the timetable with activities and budget, and do not turn the exchange into adult-led classroom teaching. A monthly Gantt does not replace the required session programme. Report weaknesses, a relevant alternative, the reasoned decision, missing data, and sources.

Write only to the assigned path and propose model changes to the coordinator. Use the applicable spreadsheet, document, or PDF workflow and its verification steps. Deliver the actual file and the checks performed; never claim an export or validation that did not occur. A fresh stage validator reviews the product; do not approve your own work.
